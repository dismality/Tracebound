How to play

Design. Predict. Trace.

  1. Shape the mission. Draw walls and costly terrain, or load a prepared scenario.
  2. Choose a strategy. Read its promise and trade-off before you run it.
  3. Inspect the search. Pause or single-step to see how the frontier changes.
  4. Race the algorithms. Predict a winner, then test your reasoning on the exact same board.

Tip: terrain costs 5 to cross. BFS, DFS, Greedy, and Bidirectional BFS treat every open cell as one step; Dijkstra and A* account for terrain cost.
